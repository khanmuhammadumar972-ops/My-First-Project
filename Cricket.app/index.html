Historic Wins

ICC WORLD CUP 🏆
MARCH 25, 1992 · MELBOURNE
ODI WORLD CUP CHAMPIONS
vs ENGLAND · WON BY 22 RUNS

Under Imran Khan's inspirational leadership, Pakistan defied all odds to win their maiden World Cup. From near-elimination, the team stormed through to glory — an iconic triumph that united a nation.

ICC T20 WC ⚡
JUNE 21, 2009 · LORDS
T20 WORLD CUP CHAMPIONS
vs SRI LANKA · WON BY 8 WICKETS

A dominant display of T20 mastery at Lord's. Pakistan chased down Sri Lanka's total with ease, with Shahid Afridi's bowling and the top order's aggression sealing a historic T20 title.

ICC CT 🥇
JUNE 18, 2017 · THE OVAL
CHAMPIONS TROPHY WINNERS
vs INDIA · WON BY 180 RUNS

The most stunning reversal in Champions Trophy history. Pakistan, ranked lowest, destroyed tournament favorites India by 180 runs in the final. Fakhar Zaman's century and Hasan Ali's bowling were breathtaking.

TEST SERIES 🎯
1987 — SUBCONTINENTAL DOMINANCE
HISTORIC TEST SERIES
vs ENGLAND — HOME & AWAY WINS

The late 1980s saw Wasim Akram and Imran Khan spearhead Pakistan's Test dominance. Reverse swing became a weapon that confounded every batting lineup in the world.

ODI CLASSIC 🏏
NOVEMBER 2003 · SHARJAH
SHARJAH DESERT STORM
vs INDIA · RECORD CHASE

Sharjah has witnessed some of Pakistan's greatest ODI moments — from Javed Miandad's last-ball six to successive desert storm victories that became part of cricket mythology.

LANDMARK 🌟
ONGOING LEGACY
REACHING NO.1 IN ALL FORMATS
TEST · ODI · T20 RANKINGS

Pakistan has held the No.1 ICC Test ranking, showcasing consistency across formats. With Babar Azam leading the modern era, the Green Titans continue to stamp their authority on world cricket.
